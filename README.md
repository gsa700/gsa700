Free software I write for my own music library and my ham radio station, shared in case it is
useful to you too. Everything here runs on Linux and Windows, and most of it on a Mac as well.

## Music

Three desktop apps for a music library you own, made to work together and each useful alone.

And without screenshots:

- **[Waverider](https://github.com/gsa700/waverider)** · Your radio and your Wavelog logbook in
  step: frequency, mode and power follow the rig, and every WSJT-X contact is logged as it happens.
- **[virtual-flex](https://github.com/gsa700/virtual-flex)** · A virtual FlexRadio on the LAN, so an
  Elecraft K4 drives a 4O3A PGXL, TGXL and AGXL stack as if it were a Flex.
- **[jbd-can-bridge](https://github.com/gsa700/jbd-can-bridge)** · A JBD battery management system
  on a Victron GX or any Pylontech-compatible inverter, read-only, with nothing installed on the GX.
- **[davis-rx](https://github.com/gsa700/davis-rx)** · A receiver for the Davis Vantage Pro2 weather
  station's 900 MHz signal on a RAK4631, served as a web page and Prometheus metrics.
